**How is progress in AI changing the companies you follow?**

New models, growing inference demand, and rising cloud capital expenditure flow through to chips, memory, optical interconnects, power, and data centers. Hone helps you connect those developments to individual companies: ask a question in plain language, explore its implications with industry research and professional financial data, and follow the companies that deserve ongoing attention.

Hone has built an **ontology-based research foundation covering around 50 AI infrastructure companies**, spanning optical communications, power, memory and storage, chips, and related segments. This foundation supports industry context and company analysis in conversations, helping research follow relationships across the supply chain.

## Turn AI headlines into research questions

You can start without choosing a tool, writing a prompt template, or preparing a research outline. Open the investment assistant and ask what matters to you:

- “How does growing inference demand affect optical modules, memory, and power?”
- “How should I think about Micron's valuation, and which operating metrics should I track?”
- “What does higher cloud capital expenditure mean for each company I follow?”
- “Does this news change the company's long-term thesis, or reflect short-term sentiment?”

Hone combines AI industry context, company research, and currently available data to help you examine transmission paths, key assumptions, and questions that need more evidence. Follow up on an answer, or use “Ask HONE” in a research panel to continue with that context.

## Follow companies and build an ongoing research routine

Add companies to your watchlist or holdings to give your research a starting point. Interpret company news, earnings developments, and relevant events in the context of their businesses and industries. Review important updates in the app's notifications, then return to the assistant for further analysis.

**Hone connects to professional financial databases** to support questions about financial statements, growth, profitability, cash flow, valuation, and market data. Move from “What happened?” to “What does it mean for the business?”, “What expectations does the valuation imply?”, and “Which data should I check next?”

For a memory company, that might mean following pricing cycles, capacity, margins, and capital expenditure. For an optical communications company, it might mean examining product transitions, customer demand, and delivery schedules.

Data coverage and refresh rates depend on the source. Feature availability also depends on account permissions and service configuration.

## Keep up with Serenity and other market voices on your phone

Open **Research → Influencer Digest** (「研究 → 大V速报」) to browse public updates from supported authors such as **Serenity / 白毛**. The live digest currently **syncs every 15 minutes**, bringing scattered updates into one timeline so you can keep up with industry discussions throughout the day.

- Read Chinese translations, expand English originals where available, and view images and publication times.
- Open the author's original post and the translation or aggregation source to check the full context.
- Expand “HONE Interpretation” to explore the distinction between opinions and facts, then continue your research with “Ask HONE.”

## Read the macro signals—and their impact on AI infrastructure

The Macro Traffic Light helps you monitor the broader market and economic cycle, review changes in financial conditions and market risk, and inspect the reasoning behind the signals. Start with the signal, open the details, or ask a follow-up question.

**Hone puts particular emphasis on how those changes affect AI infrastructure.** Interest rates, liquidity, and risk appetite matter through their effects on financing costs, cloud capital expenditure, data center construction, and upstream demand. Research also considers AI demand, commercialization, financing, and capital expenditure to examine the durability of industry growth.

“What color is today's signal?” is a starting point. The next questions are which companies are affected, how the impact travels through the industry, whether business fundamentals have changed, and what further evidence is needed.

## Start using Hone

1. Open **[the Hone user app](https://hone-claw.com/chat)**, follow the sign-in prompts, and ask your first AI or company research question.
2. Add companies you follow or hold so future updates have a clear research focus.
3. Visit Research for the Macro Traffic Light and Influencer Digest, and Notifications for updates relevant to you.
4. When something deserves a closer look, select “Ask HONE” to continue in the conversation.

Use Hone in your browser: explore in depth on a computer, or check updates and ask follow-up questions on your phone.

## Open source and self-hosting

Honeclaw is the open-source project behind Hone. Built with **Rust + SolidJS / TypeScript**, it provides the application foundation for conversations, research, notifications, and access across channels. To explore the implementation, contribute, or self-host, start here:

- [Complete wiki: installation, configuration, startup, and troubleshooting](./docs/wiki.md)
- [Repository map and module entry points](./docs/repo-map.md)
- [Development and collaboration guidelines](./AGENTS.md)
- [Releases and installation packages](https://github.com/B-M-Capital-Research/honeclaw/releases)

The professional financial databases, proprietary ontology research, and certain research assets used by the hosted product are not distributed with the public code. Self-hosting requires your own model and data service configuration; cloning the repository does not grant access to all hosted data or account features.
