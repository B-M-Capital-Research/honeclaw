Evidence is deliberately asymmetric. The optional model can classify only admitted IDs and cannot change that truth status. The OpenAI official RSS endpoint is enabled after a live host/XML check; other official source definitions are registered but stay unconfigured until their feeds are individually verified. A final local refresh retained 40 source-linked milestones, 3 confirmed and 37 clues; SOFC stayed empty rather than receiving invented filler.

The same dialog now contains a secondary “十日简报” view. It reviews only events published in the prior ten Beijing calendar days, records event-ID changes relative to the previous snapshot, and links every retained item to its original. The next ten days are represented as open verification questions with a review-by date, not as predicted events. A question without current evidence is `waiting_for_source` and cannot produce an early conclusion. Broad source titles use the matched topic sentence from the bounded excerpt so the review headline remains relevant.
